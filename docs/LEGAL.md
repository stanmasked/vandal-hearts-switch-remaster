# Legal and Asset Handling Notes

This file is practical project guidance, not legal advice.

## Upstream code

Before copying, modifying, or redistributing upstream code, identify the exact
license and its conditions in the upstream repository. Preserve required notices
and comply with the applicable license. If no clear license is present, do not
assume that public availability grants permission to reuse or redistribute code;
seek clarification or legal advice.

Upstream project:
https://github.com/HalmyLyseas/VandalHearts-PcPort

## Original game and assets

Vandal Hearts and its original game data may be protected by copyright and other
rights. A non-commercial purpose does not automatically grant permission to
redistribute copyrighted material.

- Do not commit disc images, ROMs, extracted game files, textures, music, fonts,
  or other proprietary assets.
- Do not include copyrighted assets in releases, CI artifacts, screenshots, or
  sample packages unless you have permission to do so.
- If the software requires game data, document how users can provide their own
  legally obtained files locally, subject to applicable law and the relevant
  rights.
- Do not circumvent access controls or distribute tools or instructions intended
  to facilitate unlawful acquisition of game data.

## Third-party dependencies

Maintain an inventory of third-party libraries, versions, licenses, and notices.
Verify that each dependency's license permits the intended use and distribution.

## Branding and project status

This is an independent community project and is not affiliated with or endorsed
by the original game's rights holders. Do not imply official status. Do not claim
Nintendo Switch compatibility until a build has actually been tested on the
intended environment.
